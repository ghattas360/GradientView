Straight answers

Is the free tier actually useful, or a trial?

Actually useful, forever. The wheel tracker, exposure views, IV rank, and shareable cards are free permanently — no time limit. It's the container everything else plugs into.

Why modules instead of simple tiers?

A wheel seller, a vol trader, and a multi-broker investor value almost disjoint feature sets. Modules mean you never pay for bloat. Prefer simplicity? The preset stacks behave like tiers.

What does "founding member" get me?

Lifetime pricing locked on whatever stack you choose, plus a direct line to shape the roadmap. Waitlist members also vote on final pricing before launch.

Do you sell my data or take trades?

Never. Broker connections are read-only via trusted aggregators; we never store credentials and never place trades. The platform is decision-support, not an advisor or a broker.
